FIELD NOTES
• First-aid kits restore 30 health; they can also drop from opponents.
• Ammunition satchels replenish reserve rounds.
• Blue-coated soldiers and tougher officers attack from range.
• Press 1–4 to switch between period-inspired firearms; ammo is tracked per weapon.
• Get close to an enemy and press Space or use the knife attack button for melee.
• F taunts nearby enemies; if soldiers are retreating, press F to shout “COWARDS!”
• Some Union soldiers are ambushers: they use cover to shoot, then reposition when you get close.
• Fences, stone walls, trees, and carts block movement and shots.
• Dodge several shots in a row and nearby soldiers may react in disbelief.
• After 50 Union soldiers are tagged, surviving enemies panic and retreat briefly.
• When only one soldier remains in a wave, he may panic and flee the field or fall back to cover.
• Press G to become a ghost: Union soldiers panic, retreat, and fire harmless shots that pass through you. Press G again to return to your soldier.
• Stylized, non-graphic hit effects.
