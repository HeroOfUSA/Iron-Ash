THE ASHEN CROSSING

Play as one infantryman in a fictional Civil War-inspired arcade skirmish. Move around cover, aim with your mouse, and survive incoming waves. White + markers restore health; satchels replenish ammunition.

Objective: survive waves, collect supplies, and set a high score.


CONTROLS

Tip: keep moving between shots. Use walls and trees as cover.
FIELD NOTES
• First-aid kits restore 30 health; they can also drop from opponents.
• Ammunition satchels replenish reserve rounds.
• Blue-coated soldiers and tougher officers attack from range.
• Press 1–4 to switch between period-inspired firearms; ammo is tracked per weapon.
• Get close to an enemy and press Space or use the knife attack button for melee.
• F taunts nearby enemies; they may turn toward you.
• Fences, stone walls, trees, and carts block movement and shots.
• Stylized, non-graphic blood effects.
